ComiNavi is an unofficial iPhone and iPad companion that connects Comiket catalog research with navigation at the venue.

Find circles
• Search the catalog by circle, creator, genre, and other details.
• Browse circle cuts in a visual gallery.
• For supported circles, view shinagaki information posted on X.

Navigate the venue
• See halls and placements on a Tokyo Big Sight venue map.
• Save circles, color-code them, and keep their locations easy to find.
• Optionally use location and heading to estimate where you are inside the venue.

Keep your plan together
• Sign in with Circle.ms and download available event catalogs.
• Sync saved circles and notes with your Circle.ms favorites.
• Keep catalogs and favorite lists organized by event.

Location is processed on your device only while Where Am I is open and is not sent to the ComiNavi operator. You can select a venue area manually without granting location permission.

A Circle.ms account and a supported catalog available from Circle.ms are required. The first catalog download may transfer a large amount of data.
