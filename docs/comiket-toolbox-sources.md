details can change; the app links to the live source instead of treating this
snapshot as authoritative.

## C108 notices represented in the app

- C108 runs on 2026-08-15 and 2026-08-16 at Tokyo Big Sight.
- East Halls 4–6 are unavailable during renovation.
- The East area has no operating convenience store for C108; obtain drinks
  before entering or use vending/catering options.
- Every general attendee needs an admission ticket or wristband; only PM entry
  wristbands are offered on site.
- Crowds, queues, entrances, and access instructions can change. The toolbox
  tells attendees to follow current signs and staff instead of presenting a
  static indoor route.

## Product boundaries

- Resource descriptions summarize why a link is useful, not its full contents.
- Checklist state is private to the device and is not uploaded.
- The venue action opens public-transit directions to Tokyo Big Sight only.
- ComiNavi does not claim that a particular internal walking path is available.
